About the Tournament

The tournament was first played on 26/12/2021. Held approximately every six months, it features 4 card games — Monopoly, Bluff, Spoon & UNO — each played twice, totalling 8 games per edition.

Scoring: 2 points for 1st place, 1 point for 2nd. Most points wins — ties broken by game wins.
